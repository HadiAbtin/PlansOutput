# 12-Week 10K Training Program

## Program Overview

This is a comprehensive 12-week periodized training program designed to build you from a 25 km/week aerobic base to peak 10K fitness. The program is structured in four distinct phases: Base Building (Weeks 1-4), Build (Weeks 5-9), Peak (Weeks 10-11), and Taper (Week 12).

**Your Goal**: Sub-50:00 10K finish on March 20, 2026

**Current Fitness**: 5K time trial of 24:30 (24:30 equivalent pace)

**Program Structure**: 4 sessions per week with progressive mileage (25-30 km starting → 35-40 km peak → 20-25 km taper)

---

## Training Zones Reference

### Zone 1: Easy (Conversational Pace)
- **Intensity**: RPE 3-4 (out of 10)
- **Description**: You can speak in full sentences; low stress on body
- **Purpose**: Building aerobic base, recovery runs, long runs

### Zone 2: Moderate (Slightly Elevated Effort)
- **Intensity**: RPE 5-6 (out of 10)
- **Description**: Can speak short sentences; building aerobic capacity
- **Purpose**: Steady efforts, final portion of long runs

### Zone 3: Hard (Race Intensity)
- **Intensity**: RPE 7-8 (out of 10)
- **Description**: Challenging effort; breathing elevated; sustainable for 10-20 minutes
- **Purpose**: Tempo work, interval repeats, race-pace training

---

## General Nutrition Principles

### Daily Fueling Structure
- **Pre-Workout** (2-3 hours before): Carbohydrate-based meal with protein (e.g., oatmeal with banana and almond butter, or toast with peanut butter)
- **Post-Workout** (within 30-60 minutes): Carbs and protein combination to support recovery (e.g., yogurt with granola, smoothie with fruit, or sandwich with cheese)
- **Throughout Day**: Balanced meals with adequate vegetarian protein sources (legumes, lentils, tofu, tempeh, nuts, seeds, dairy)

### Hydration Guidelines
- **Daily**: Aim for 2-3 liters of water spread throughout the day
- **Pre-Run**: 200-300 ml of water 30 minutes before
- **During Run**: Sip every 20 minutes if running longer than 60 minutes (150-200 ml per sip)
- **Post-Run**: 500-750 ml within 30 minutes to rehydrate

### Vegetarian Protein Targets
- **Daily intake**: 1.2-1.6 g per kg of body weight (approximately 96-128 g for an 80 kg athlete)
- **Sources**: Legumes, lentils, tofu, tempeh, nuts, seeds, dairy products, plant-based protein powder

# Week 1 - Base Building

## Week Overview

---

## Day 1 - Monday

### Workout Plan

**Warm-up**
- 5 minutes easy jogging to loosen up

**Main Workout**
- Type: Easy run
- Distance/Time: 5-6 km
- Intensity: RPE 3-4 (conversational pace)
- Notes: Focus on finding a comfortable rhythm. You should be able to speak in full sentences throughout.

**Cool-down**
- 5 minutes easy walking and light stretching

### Nutrition Plan

**Pre-Workout**
- Eat a light breakfast 2-3 hours before your run (oatmeal with banana or toast with peanut butter)
- Drink a glass of water 30 minutes before

**Post-Workout**
- Have a recovery snack within 30-60 minutes: yogurt with granola, or a protein smoothie with fruit
- Drink water to rehydrate

**Meals**
- Breakfast: Oatmeal with berries and nuts (pre-workout fuel)
- Lunch: Lentil and vegetable soup with whole grain bread
- Dinner: Chickpea curry with rice and vegetables
- Snacks: Mixed nuts, fruit, or hummus with veggie sticks

### Hydration
- Drink water throughout the day
- This is an easy run under 6 km, so water before and after is sufficient

### Recovery
- Spend 5-10 minutes stretching major muscle groups (calves, hamstrings, quads, hip flexors)
- Get 7-8 hours of sleep tonight to support adaptation
- Light activity or rest today sets the foundation for consistency
